COACHCARE PRISM

Rethinking remote care: redesigning legacy clinical workflows so care teams can focus on the patients who need them most.

CoachCare had grown into a powerful remote-care platform, but that growth came with baggage. Years of features, alerts, patient data, and clinical workflows were spread across three legacy platforms and several third-party tools, making it harder for care teams to quickly understand what needed their attention.

With Prism, I restructured the experience around a task-driven workflow, surfacing the context clinicians needed when they needed it and using AI to reduce the cognitive burden of navigating complex patient information. The new system also helped turn more of the work care teams were already doing into structured, trackable activity instead of time lost to workflow friction.

The goal was simple: help care teams spend less time figuring out the system, and more time caring for patients.
