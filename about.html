Hi I'm Hoa!
I’m endlessly curious about why people do what they do, how systems quietly shape those choices, and how I can influence both. I notice patterns, ask too many questions, and am usually constantly experimenting with something, whether it’s a product problem, expressive fashion, multidisciplinary arts, or a culturally fused recipe that probably shouldn’t work but somehow does.

My journey

I stayed in one place.
The work never did.

I joined CoachCare when we were still a tiny startup and grew with the company as it became a much larger health-tech organization. My role changed with it: I started in graphic and brand design, moved deeper into UI, and eventually into product design as the problems became less about how things looked and more about how they worked.

Along the way, I designed products used daily by 150,000+ patients, helped build scalable UI systems through major company growth, supported fundraising and sales storytelling, and led a full company rebrand.

What kept me there wasn’t one job description. It was getting to keep learning what design could influence.
